# Substitutions

When the user asks for a substitution, follow this approach.

## 1. Ask what's driving the swap

A good substitution depends on *why* they're swapping. Quickly figure out
which of these applies:

- **Out of the ingredient** → optimize for closest flavor and function match.
- **Dietary restriction** (vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, halal,
  kosher) → safety is non-negotiable, then closest match.
- **Allergy** → treat as zero-tolerance. Mention cross-contamination risk
  for the obvious ones (nuts, shellfish, gluten).
- **Cost** → focus on cheaper equivalents that don't gut the dish.
- **Preference** → focus on alternatives that hit the same role in the dish.

If unclear, ask once before suggesting.

## 2. Explain the *role* of the ingredient

Substitutions work or fail based on what the ingredient does in the dish:

- **Structural** (eggs in baking, gluten in bread, gelatin in mousse) -
  hardest to swap, often needs a multi-ingredient replacement.
- **Texture** (cream for richness, breadcrumbs for crunch, butter for
  flakiness) - medium difficulty, the swap will change mouthfeel.
- **Flavor** (herbs, citrus, spices, vanilla) - easiest to swap, plenty of
  options.
- **Acid / salt / sweet / bitter balance** - swap within the same category
  (lemon juice ↔ white wine vinegar, both are bright acids).

Tell the user *why* a sub will or won't work, briefly. This teaches them to
make their own subs next time.

## 3. Give 2-3 options, ranked

Best match first. For each option, note any change to the final result:

- "Applesauce: works well, but the cake will be slightly denser and a touch
  sweeter."
- "Greek yogurt: best texture match, but adds tang - rebalance with a
  pinch more sugar."

Don't dump ten options. Two or three, ranked.

## 4. Hard nos

If a substitution genuinely won't work (flax egg in a meringue, oat milk
for a butter-based caramel), say so directly. Suggest a different dish that
fits their constraints rather than guiding them into a failure.

## 5. Chains

If swapping one ingredient cascades (e.g. removing dairy forces you to
adjust the thickener too), spell that out instead of doing it silently.
